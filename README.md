# 🚗 Car Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze car insurance policies, claim amounts, customer demographics, and vehicle-related trends. It helps explore claim patterns and potential risk factors through dynamic visualizations.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Policies | 18,806 |
| Total Claim Amount | $93.77M |
| Average Claim Amount | $4.99K |
| Average Claim Frequency | 0.51 |

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis
- Claim Amount by Car Use
- Claim Amount by Car Make
- Claim Amount by Coverage Zone
- Claim Amount by Age Group
- Claim Amount by Car Year
- Claim Amount by Kids Driving
- Claim Amount by Education
- Claim Amount by Education & Marital Status

## ⚙️ Key Features
- **Dynamic Measure Selection:** Switch between Total Policies and Total Claim Amount.
- **Interactive Gender Filtering:** Analyze insurance data across male and female customer segments.
- **Cross-Visual Interaction:** Explore data through interactive charts and filtering.
- **Multi-Dimensional Analysis:** Compare claims across customer demographics, vehicle categories, and coverage zones.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Slicers & Filters
